Built for players and creators

Features

A growing set of ways to replay Halo CE, make new experiences, and bring Custom Edition content into the game.

Game data

Maps Looking…

The maps are copied out of your own disc image (an .iso of Halo: Combat Evolved for the Xbox) into this browser's storage for this site. Nothing is uploaded, and the site serves no game data.

Custom Edition maps Looking…

A custom_maps folder: Halo Custom Edition maps, in it or in its subfolders (each a category in the game's map lists), with Custom Edition's bitmaps.map, sounds.map and loc.map in the folder itself. Or maps chosen alone, or a zip of them or of a custom_maps folder (as Export makes). They are added to those already here, the maps downloaded from hosts among them.

MCC packages

Add a Halo 1 MCC package folder (a Workshop item's, or your own maps). Files stay in this browser, kept apart from your Xbox maps and your Custom Edition maps.

Looking…

Existing files are never replaced. Quit games in other tabs before importing.

Find imported maps in the game's MCC SINGLEPLAYER and MCC MULTIPLAYER lists. Campaign progression uses the MCC Campaigns setting. A finished import is checked again by the game before loading.

Mods

No mods yet.

    A mod is a folder with a mod.toml and a tags folder of Halo PC tag files: sounds, textures and text that play over the maps' for you alone. Each is played once added: the game's main menu's Mods turns them off and sets the order they load in.

    Tag profiles

    No tag profiles yet.

      The Tag Injector's profiles (the game's Settings, Gameplay, Tag Injector), with their tags, so that they play without the maps their tags came from. A profile's export has the multiplayer and campaign profiles that play it; Export all has every one. Import takes such a zip, or a profile's folder or the tag_injector folder of another build's data, and replaces a profile of the same name.

      Play together

      Play system link with friends in their browsers: create a room and send them its link. Each of you then finds the others' games under Multiplayer.

      The browsers of a room connect to each other directly. They find each other through public message brokers, which see nothing of the room: what they pass on is sealed with the link's secret. The Linux, Windows and Android builds cannot join a room yet.

      Servers

      Looking…

        The official master servers list dedicated servers and the games players host for the internet. Each list is signed, and this page checks it before it shows a game. The listed servers are not contacted: Play here starts the game and joins the one you choose.

          About

          Halo Content Evolved is an independent project built on OpenCE, an open-source port based on the Halo: Combat Evolved Xbox decompilation. It began in Thelinkin3000's Switch-focused fork and continues as Halo Content Evolved.

          The aim is a feature-rich Halo CE: theater, forge, firefight, social features, scoring, skulls, a campaign randomizer, and easy access to the Custom Edition content community has made over the last 20 years.
