Steam Workshop test

A test page: it signs in to Steam, checks that the account has Halo: Combat Evolved Anniversary in The Master Chief Collection, and downloads its resource maps and Halo 1 Workshop items as files.

Relay

Looking for the relay…

Steam refuses connections from web pages that are not its own, so this page talks to Steam through a small relay on your computer. In the game's repository, run:

node tools/steam_relay.mjs

and open http://localhost:8791/testing/workshop/, or keep this page and press Retry.

Steam account

Not signed in.

Open the Steam app on your phone, choose the shield (Steam Guard), then Scan a QR code.

On the phone itself: open the sign-in link.

The sign-in is kept in this tab only, and goes when the tab closes. Steam lists it under Steam Guard > Manage devices, where it can be removed.
